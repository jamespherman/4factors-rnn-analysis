# Audit of January–February 2026 work (October 2026)

Problems found while regenerating the R01 recorded-vs-RNN selectivity figure,
and what was done about each. Work is on branch `restore-lost-work`.

## 1. Merge 6566a7d discarded a week of code and data

The Jan 29 merge of `6fa65af` (Jan 22–29 work) with `138b2fc` (Jan 22) kept the
Jan 22 versions of `src/model.py`, `src/losses.py`, `src/data_loader.py`,
`scripts/train_model.py` and `data/*.mat`. The checkpoints in
`results/final_model`, `results/replication` and `results/conditioned_loss_08_15`
no longer loaded, and `data/` reverted to an earlier export (08_15: 14 E / 28 I
instead of 41 E / 13 I). The Jan 29 – Feb 1 work (input restructuring,
biological inputs, audit) rebuilt parts of the model on top of the reverted code
and ran on the stale export.

**Fixed (eb069c8):** code restored from `6fa65af`; `arch_version='feb2026'`
reproduces the rebuilt model so Feb checkpoints load (verified bit-identical
outputs against both original code versions); canonical exports restored
(identical to `4factors-analysis-pipeline/data/rnn_exports/`); stale exports kept
in `data/export_2026-01-22_0631/` and the Feb scripts point there.

## 2. Recorded interneurons were fit by excitatory units

All training scripts fit recorded neuron *j* with model unit *j*
(`model_rates[:, :, :n_recorded]`). Recorded E and I neurons are interleaved in the
export, while model units `0..n_exc-1` are excitatory, so:

| Session | Recorded I fit by E units | Model I units fit to any recorded neuron |
|---|---|---|
| 08_13 | 4/4 | 0/4 |
| 08_14 | 16/16 | 0/16 |
| 08_15 | 12/13 | 2/13 |

The Feb experiments have the same problem (08_15 stale export: 42 recorded
neurons all fit by E units). The model's I units were therefore unconstrained by
interneuron data, and every I→E analysis to date (`ie_connectivity_analysis_report.md`,
`phase6_replication_report.md`, the Feb connectivity reports) analyzed them as if
they were the recorded interneurons.

**Fixed:** `scripts/train_session.py` maps recorded E neurons to E units and recorded
I neurons to I units, and stores the mapping as `recorded_unit_indices` in
`config.json`. Models retrained in `results/retrained/`.

## 3. Phase 2 connectivity analysis paired rates with the wrong trials

`scripts/analyze_connectivity.py` takes factor labels from the *last* 20% of trials,
but the saved validation rates come from `train_val_split` (random, stratified by
reward). It also assumes recorded neurons are ordered E then I, and treats salience
as a numeric regressor. The near-zero selectivity it reports (partial eta² ≈
0.002–0.005) is consistent with labels unrelated to the rates.

**Replaced** by `scripts/analyze_ie_structure.py` (selectivity measured on the
recordings with pipeline-matched methods; permutation nulls; seeds and sessions).

## 4. Salience labels were misread

Export coding is `trial_salience` 0 = image trial (not applicable), 1 = high,
2 = low. `scripts/compare_recorded_vs_rnn_selectivity.py` and
`scripts/compute_r01_analyses.py` treated 2 as high and 0 as low, so their
"salience" contrast was low-salience bullseyes vs face/non-face images.
`RNNDataset.get_salience_binary` median-splits the three values, so the
condition-specific loss's "salience" factor was low-salience bullseye vs everything
else.

**Fixed** in the new analysis (`src/analysis/pipeline_selectivity.py`) and the new
training conditions (stimulus class × location).

## 5. Selectivity measure didn't match the neuronal pipeline

The R01 figure averaged rates over the whole trial (fixation to after reward),
pooled all target locations, and compared location 1 vs 2–4 under a
"pref vs non-pref" label. The Feb ROC analysis used a single median target-onset
bin, though target onset varies by ~0.5 s across trials.

**Fixed:** `src/analysis/pipeline_selectivity.py` reproduces
`analyze_window_roc.m`: 50–250 ms after each trial's own target onset, trials at
each neuron's preferred location, pipeline contrasts (salience on bullseye trials,
identity on image trials, probability at location 1 or 3), ≥5 trials per condition.
One deviation: no saccade times in the export, so the pipeline's saccade-based
fallback for preferred location is replaced by the visual maximum.

## 6. Reward and probability are not in the model inputs

Reward size and block (probability) are not model inputs before reward delivery,
and stimulus/location barely predict reward (accuracy 0.56–0.65 vs chance
0.55–0.60). Visual-epoch reward selectivity cannot be fit with the current inputs;
probability only to the extent it follows from location. Adding task-context
inputs is the design question in `INPUT_RESTRUCTURING_SPEC.md` and is left open.

## 7. Target onset in the input channels

Per trial, onset is recovered from `input_target_loc` (first bin at or after
`targetOn - fixOn`). Trials where the channel never turns on (`targetOn` NaN) or is
on from the first bin (target before the exported window) are excluded from
visual-epoch analyses: 343/412, 457/536 and 621/1043 trials remain. The fixation
channel is missing on 30–45% of trials (NaN `fixOff`), which affects model inputs
but not these analyses; worth checking in `export_for_rnn.m`.
