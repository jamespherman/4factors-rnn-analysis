# Retrained models: results (October 2026)

Models retrained with the corrected neuron-to-unit mapping (recorded E → E units,
recorded I → I units; see `AUDIT_2026-10.md` §2) using `scripts/train_session.py`.
Three sessions (Newton 08_13, 08_14, 08_15) × two variants × seeds 42, 43, 44, in
`results/retrained/{variant}/Newton_{session}_2025_SC_seed{seed}/`.

- `cond`: L_trial + conditioned PSTH loss over 16 conditions (stimulus class ×
  location; stimulus class = face / non-face / high-salience bullseye /
  low-salience bullseye).
- `cond_target`: `cond` plus a target-aligned conditioned PSTH loss (−100 to
  +400 ms around each trial's target onset).

- Spatial selectivity is reproduced on held-out trials by every well-fit retrained
  model, in pattern and magnitude (mean |sel| 0.30–0.43 vs 0.41 recorded). The
  legacy models do not reproduce it.
- Identity selectivity is not reproduced consistently (r −0.07 to +0.26), though
  recorded identity selectivity is modestly reliable.
- Salience: recorded selectivity barely replicates across trial halves, so this
  panel cannot discriminate between models. The negative r in `cond_target` s44
  (−0.38) does not appear in the other seeds.
- Reward and probability selectivity are reliable in the recordings but cannot be
  produced by the model without task-context inputs (`INPUT_RESTRUCTURING_SPEC.md`).

## Recurrent weight structure

`scripts/analyze_ie_structure.py`; full tables in
`results/retrained/ie_structure/{variant}_report.md`.

- **No factor-specific I→E structure replicates across seeds.** Of 9 I→E tests ×
  3 seeds × 2 variants, one survives FDR (identity targeting, `cond_target` s43,
  z = 2.93); the other seeds give z = 1.78 and 1.02 (same sign, n.s.).
- **The I→E weights are not identified by the data.** Correlation of the same
  recorded-unit weights between seeds is ~0 for 08_14 and 08_15 (I→E −0.01 to
  0.06) and 0.13–0.25 for 08_13, although the seeds fit equally well. Equally good
  fits with unrelated I→E weights mean that "no structure" in one fit is not
  evidence that the circuit lacks structure.
- E→E spatial like-to-like connectivity is positive in all six model sets
  (z 0.55–2.24), weak but consistent.

## Implication for the core hypothesis

The current fits cannot support the claim that I→E weights are unstructured,
because they do not pin the I→E weights down. Before interpreting I→E structure,
it needs to be shown that structure *can* be recovered: e.g. a ground-truth test
that trains on data generated by a model with planted factor-specific I→E
connectivity (matched neuron counts and trial numbers) and checks whether the
analysis detects it. Constraining the weights more strongly (more sessions,
regularization, or perturbation data) may be needed.
